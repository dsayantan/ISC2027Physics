The Heritage School · Class 12 · First Term 2026–27

Physics First Term

Two slide decks that work as a pair. The first teaches the physics — the concepts in the order they build, the derivations, and every type of problem that gets set. The second tests it, with problems from six sources and worked solutions. Both hide the answer until you ask for it, so you can attempt each one first.

The chapter list is from FirstTermSyllabus.txt; it is a school decision and cannot be read off the CISCE syllabus, which is organised into nine units for the whole year. Optics is one syllabus unit but is set out here as two sections, Ray and Wave, which is why seven sections cover six chapters. Two things worth knowing before you reach for an older paper: polarisation is no longer in the Wave Optics syllabus — Malus’ law, Brewster’s law and polaroids were all set in the 2018, 2019 and 2020 board papers but are now out of scope; and these six chapters carry 50 of the 70 board marks, scaled up to 70 for the First Term. Scope was checked against Syllabus/ISC-2027.pdf, not assumed from the papers.

How the two decks are kept in step

The concepts deck does not write its own list of problem types: it imports them from the same modules the problem deck is built from, so a type cannot be named one thing in one deck and something else in the other. Both builders check this on every run.

Where the material comes from

Concepts follow the ISC 2027 syllabus and what the board, specimen and school papers actually set. Problems are drawn from six sources — the NCERT Exemplar and NCERT textbook, the ISC board and specimen papers, CISCE’s competency booklet and the Heritage School papers — and each names its own. Every numerical answer was recomputed independently rather than copied across.

Both decks work the same way

Move with ← → or the buttons, s to reveal, d to mark done; the contents bar jumps to a section, the address bar tracks the slide, and your place is remembered. Printing lays the whole deck out with everything revealed, so either one doubles as a booklet.
