The chapter list is from FirstTermSyllabus.txt; it is a school decision and cannot be read off the CISCE syllabus, which is organised into nine units for the whole year. Optics is one syllabus unit but is set out here as two sections, Ray and Wave, which is why seven sections cover six chapters. Two things worth knowing before you reach for an older paper: polarisation is no longer in the Wave Optics syllabus — Malus’ law, Brewster’s law and polaroids were all set in the 2018, 2019 and 2020 board papers but are now out of scope; and these six chapters carry 50 of the 70 board marks, scaled up to 70 for the First Term, so the weighting is not the board weighting. Scope was checked against Syllabus/ISC-2027.pdf, not assumed from the papers.

Practice papers

Three papers on the ISC Physics Paper 1 pattern: four sections A–D, twenty questions, 70 marks over three hours. Section A is one question with fourteen one-mark sub-parts — the first seven multiple choice or assertion–reason, the rest very short answer. Sections B, C and D carry internal choice in exactly two questions each. Every paper ends with its own answer key, and each of the three covers all six chapters, so they differ in emphasis rather than in scope.

Question 20 is a passage-based question in all three, which is how ISC has set the last five-marker since 2023: a short piece of applied prose followed by five one-mark parts. Each paper also prints the standard table of useful constants, so you are not expected to remember ε0 or me.

Where the questions come from

The revision page draws on six sources: the NCERT Exemplar (Exemplar/) and the NCERT textbook (NCERT/), the ISC board and specimen papers in Question-Papers/ and Specimen-Papers/, the CISCE competency-focused booklet, and the Heritage School First Term paper of 2024–25. Each problem names its own source, so you can always go back to the original. The three practice papers and the unit test are written fresh against the blueprint rather than assembled from past questions.

A note on the school paper

Worth knowing if you are tempted to treat last year’s First Term paper as a guide: its questions were taken almost entirely from the ISC 2025 specimen paper and the 2020 and 2023 board papers, and none from NCERT or the Exemplar. Three of its questions were off the First Term syllabus. That is the opposite of the pattern in ../Maths/, and it is why the revision set here is built around covering concepts rather than around predicting a paper.

Answers

Every numerical answer on the revision page and in the three answer keys was recomputed independently rather than copied across — src/verify_papers.py runs the ninety checks covering the papers and prints each computed value beside the printed one. Exemplar answers are pinned to the printed key in Exemplar/leep117.pdf.

Also in this folder

Check the current syllabus in Syllabus/ before working through the older papers in Question-Papers/; it decides what is still examinable, and the Optics chapter in particular has shrunk. Specimen-Papers/ holds the CISCE specimen sets, which are the best single guide to how a question will be worded.

Rebuilding

python3 src/build_revision.py rebuilds the revision page and prints the section, type and problem counts it wrote — the chips above should be kept in step with them. python3 src/build_papers.py rebuilds all three papers, or pass a number for one of them — u1 for the unit test; it refuses to write a paper that does not total 70 marks or that has the wrong number of internal choices. python3 src/verify_papers.py re-checks the arithmetic, and python3 src/verify_revision.py --print drives the deck in a headless browser to check the navigation, the per-chapter templates and the print path.
