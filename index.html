Chapter order follows Understanding ISC Mathematics (M.L. Aggarwal). Worth knowing before you reach for last year’s paper: the 2024–25 First Term paper examined Probability, Vectors, 3-D Geometry and Linear Regression, none of which is in this year’s First Term — and linear regression has been dropped from the ISC syllabus altogether. Integrals and Application of Integrals are new to the First Term and were not on that paper at all.

Practice papers

Three papers on the ISC 2027 pattern, which is new this year: four sections A–D, twenty questions, all compulsory — the old choice between Section B and Section C is gone. Each paper is 80 marks over three hours, with internal choice in three questions in each of Sections B, C and D, and an answer key on the last pages.

Where the questions come from

Problems are drawn from the NCERT Exemplar and the NCERT textbook, the ISC board and specimen papers held in Question-Papers/ and Specimen-Papers/, and the Heritage School First Term paper of 2024–25. Each problem on the revision page names its source. Where a school-paper question was ambiguous or misprinted it has been restated and the change noted.

Answers

Every numerical and algebraic answer on the revision page and in the three answer keys was checked against a computer algebra system rather than copied across.
